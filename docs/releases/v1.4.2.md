# DeskBox 1.4.2

DeskBox 1.4.2 focuses on correctness and tactile polish across Todo, Quick Capture, music, and widget groups. Task completion now remains attached to the intended item, image clipboard records behave as images from capture through copy, attachments share one visual component, and rapid circular group scrolling no longer stalls behind time-based input blockers.

## Highlights

- **Todo checks stay on the intended task.** Completion updates are item-based, including custom sorting, filters, recycled rows, and removal from the Completed view.
- **Quick Capture treats clipboard images as images.** Both list and detail panes show thumbnails, and copying an image record writes bitmap content instead of a local path.
- **Attachments share one component.** Todo and Quick Capture use equal square tiles with image thumbnails or file icons, horizontal scrolling, and hover removal.
- **Native-feeling music transport.** Filled play, pause, previous, and next icons share one responsive size; cover mode receives a taller frosted control strip and clearer single-line information layout.
- **Continuous circular group navigation.** Rapid wheel and precision-touchpad input is no longer blocked by stacked cooldowns. Effective-step accumulation, latest-target cancellation, and first/last looping remain.
- **Empty, Markdown, search, and layer states are safer.** Records and Pinned retain their empty views, Pinned creates pinned content directly, Markdown images and rich blocks lay out more reliably, and search/layer maintenance avoids stale or unbounded work.

## 中文说明

DeskBox 1.4.2 集中修复待办、随记、音乐和格子组中会直接影响操作感受的问题。待办勾选现在始终对应实际任务；剪贴板图片从采集、缩略图展示到再次复制都保持图片语义；附件统一使用同一组件；格子组快速循环滚动也不会再被时间限制卡住。

## 更新亮点

- **待办勾选对应正确任务。** 自定义排序、筛选、列表行复用以及从“已完成”移出时，都按任务本身更新。
- **随记剪贴板图片保持图片语义。** 左侧列表和右侧详情都显示缩略图，再次复制时写入图片，而不是本地路径。
- **附件统一为同一组件。** 待办与随记使用等尺寸方块，图片展示缩略图，文件展示图标，支持横向滚动和悬浮删除。
- **音乐控制更接近 Windows 原生。** 播放、暂停、上一首和下一首使用同尺寸面性图标；封面模式增加更高的磨砂控制条，并整理为清晰的单行信息布局。
- **格子组可以连续循环切换。** 快速滚轮和触控板输入不再被双重冷却拦截，同时保留有效步长合并、最终目标优先与首尾循环。
- **空状态、Markdown、搜索和层级更可靠。** 随记与固定标签保持正确空状态，固定页可直接创建固定内容，Markdown 图片与复杂块布局更稳定，搜索和桌面层维护不会留下无界或过期工作。
